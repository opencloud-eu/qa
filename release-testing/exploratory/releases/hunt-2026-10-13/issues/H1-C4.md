Tiles view: Shift+ArrowUp after Shift+ArrowDown deselects the tile the cursor moves to

## Steps to reproduce
1. Folder with ≥ 15 files, tiles view (5 per row).
2. Click empty space, ArrowRight (f000 selected), Shift+ArrowDown twice (f000–f010 selected).
3. Shift+ArrowUp once.

## Actual
f000–f004 selected; f005 — the focused tile — is not selected. Same in the opposite direction.

## Expected
f000–f005 selected (anchor to cursor, like table view).

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
`packages/web-app-files/src/composables/keyboardActions/useKeyboardFileNavigation.ts:237-245` (`handleTilesShiftUpAction`) also removes `nextIndex`; same in `handleTilesShiftDownAction` (:255-263).

## Fix idea
Stop the removal loop before `nextIndex`.

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), alan, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H1-C4, reproduced 2/2.

Screenshots: `screens/H1-C4-h1-14-run1-one-up.png` in the hunt folder.
