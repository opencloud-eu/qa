One Ctrl+Z restores every item deleted in the last 7 seconds

## Steps to reproduce
1. Delete `x1.txt`, then within 7 s delete `x2.txt` (two toasts with Undo).
2. Click an empty area of the list and press Ctrl+Z once.

## Actual
Both files are restored and both toasts disappear.

## Expected
Only the last deletion is undone.

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
Every delete binds its own Ctrl+Z handler (`packages/web-app-files/src/composables/actions/useFileActionsDeleteResources.ts:111-123`) and `packages/web-pkg/src/composables/keyboardActions/useKeyboardActions.ts:125-134` runs all matching bindings.

## Fix idea
Keep a single "last undo" binding (remove the previous one when binding a new one).

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), alan, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H2-1, reproduced 2/2.
