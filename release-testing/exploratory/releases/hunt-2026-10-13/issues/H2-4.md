Retry after a failed folder creation in a folder upload spins forever and uploads nothing

## Steps to reproduce
1. Folder upload of `up2/` (with `top.txt`, `sub/f.txt`) while the MKCOL for `up2` fails (5xx / network).
2. Panel: "Upload failed". Click "Retry all failed uploads" (network OK again).

## Actual
The panel shows "Preparing upload…" forever, no request is sent, the close button is gone. After the next upload the panel hangs on "Finalizing upload…".

## Expected
The folder and its files are uploaded on retry, or retry is not offered for folder errors.

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
`packages/web-app-files/src/HandleUpload.ts:409-421` removes the folder's files from Uppy and only reports the folder; `packages/web-runtime/src/components/UploadInfo.vue:522-536` raises the counters and calls `uppy.retryAll()`, which has nothing to retry.

## Fix idea
Re-run the folder upload on retry, or don't offer retry for folder errors / reset counters when nothing is retried.

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), alan, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H2-4, reproduced 2/2.

Screenshots: `screens/H2-4-H2-fold2-mkcol-after-retry-10s.png` in the hunt folder.
