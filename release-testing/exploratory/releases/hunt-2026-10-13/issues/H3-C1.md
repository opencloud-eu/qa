Share role dropdown shows the new role although the update failed

## Steps to reproduce
1. Share a folder with lynn as "Can view".
2. Change her role to "Can edit" while the PATCH fails (network error, or the share was removed elsewhere → 500).

## Actual
Toast "Error while editing the share." but the dropdown shows "Can edit"; the server still has "Can view" until the panel is remounted.

## Expected
The dropdown keeps showing the real role.

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
`packages/web-app-files/src/components/SideBar/Shares/Collaborators/RoleDropdown.vue:146-149` sets `selectedRole` before emitting; `Collaborators/ListItem.vue:257-297` never resets it, and `selectedRole` doesn't watch `existingShareRole`.

## Fix idea
Derive the dropdown value from the share (or revert on error).

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), alan, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H3-C1, reproduced 2/2.

Screenshots: `screens/H3-C1-H3-c1-role-change-failed.png` in the hunt folder.
