A link to a deleted or unknown space silently opens the personal space

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open a project space, note the URL.
2. Disable and delete the space, open the URL again.

## Actual
Redirect to the personal space without any message.

## Expected
"Not found / no access" as for a disabled space.

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
`DriveResolver.vue:3` renders `drive-redirect` whenever no space matches; `DriveRedirect.vue:39-47` always redirects to the personal space.

## Fix idea
Only redirect for the personal fake alias / empty alias, otherwise show not-found.

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), margaret, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H5-C3, reproduced explorer, several runs.
