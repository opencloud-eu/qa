Create app token: after a failed request, focus leaves the modal and Escape no longer closes it

## Steps to reproduce
1. Account → App Tokens → New, fill note and date.
2. Confirm while `POST /auth-app/tokens` fails; press Escape.

## Actual
Focus is on the "New" button behind the backdrop; Escape does nothing; only X closes the modal.

## Expected
Focus stays in the modal; Escape closes it.

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
`packages/web-runtime/src/components/Modals/AppTokenModal.vue` disables the focused confirm button while creating; focus is not restored.

## Fix idea
Restore focus into the modal after the request (or don't disable the focused element).

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), alan, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H9-3, reproduced 2/2.
