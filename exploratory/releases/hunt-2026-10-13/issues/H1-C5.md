Selection survives a page change; batch actions on page 2 also hit invisible items of page 1

## Steps to reproduce
1. Folder with > 20 files, 20 items per page.
2. Tick two files on page 1, go to page 2 (bar still says "2 selected", nothing visible ticked).
3. Tick one file and click Delete.

## Actual
All three files go to the trash, including the two that are not visible. Same for Move/Copy/Download. Undo is offered.

## Expected
A page change clears the selection, or the UI says that items on other pages are included.

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
The selection lives in the resources store and is not reset on a `page` query change (`packages/web-pkg/src/composables/pagination/usePagination.ts:87-88`).

## Fix idea
Reset the selection on page change, or show "n selected on other pages".

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), alan, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H1-C5, reproduced 2/2.
