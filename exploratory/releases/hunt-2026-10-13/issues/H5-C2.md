Hiding disabled spaces keeps a hidden space selected; batch Delete/Enable acts on it

## Steps to reproduce
1. Spaces overview with "Show disabled Spaces" on; tick a disabled space.
2. Turn "Show disabled Spaces" off.

## Actual
The space disappears but the bar still says "1 selected" with Edit quota / Enable / Delete; Delete opens the dialog for the invisible space.

## Expected
Hidden spaces are removed from the selection.

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
`packages/web-app-files/src/views/spaces/Projects.vue:269-276` filters only the displayed list; `setAreDisabledSpacesShown` (`web-pkg/src/composables/piniaStores/resources.ts:176`) doesn't touch the selection (same pattern for hidden files, :164).

## Fix idea
On toggle, drop selected ids that are no longer shown.

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), margaret, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H5-C2, reproduced 2/2.
