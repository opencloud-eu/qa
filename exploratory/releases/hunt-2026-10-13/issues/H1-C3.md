Cut/paste or move that fails with a network error reports nothing and empties the clipboard

## Steps to reproduce
1. Select a file, Ctrl+X, open another folder.
2. Press Ctrl+V while the `MOVE` request fails at network level (offline / connection reset; simulated with `route.abort()`).

## Actual
No message, nothing moved, and the clipboard is cleared (Paste is gone). Same for drag & drop.

## Expected
"Failed to move …" and the clipboard keeps the item so the user can retry.

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
`packages/web-client/src/webdav/client/dav.ts:231-233` assumes `error.response` exists; the catch in `packages/web-pkg/src/composables/webWorkers/pasteWorker/worker.ts:81-88` reads `e.response.headers` and throws again, so the item is neither in `successful` nor `failed`; `useFileActionsPaste.ts:169` clears the clipboard unconditionally.

## Fix idea
Handle a missing `response` in dav.ts and the worker, count the item as failed, and only clear the clipboard on success.

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), alan, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H1-C3, reproduced 2/2.
