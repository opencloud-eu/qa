Enable/Disable sync on a mixed selection fails with an error although one share was changed

## Steps to reproduce
1. Shared with me: one synced and one unsynced share.
2. Select both and click "Enable sync" (or "Disable sync").

## Actual
The call for the share already in the target state fails with 409; the UI shows "Failed to enable/disable sync…", selection stays, although the other share was changed on the server.

## Expected
The action is applied to the shares where it applies, success message.

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
`packages/web-app-files/src/composables/actions/files/useFileActionsEnableSync.ts:35-57` and `useFileActionsDisableSync.ts:35-54` iterate over all selected resources instead of only the ones that need the change.

## Fix idea
Filter the resources by `syncEnabled` before calling the API.

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), lynn, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H3-C4, reproduced 2/2 enable, 1/1 disable.
