Shared with me: "Shares / Hidden Shares" switch does not follow browser Back/Forward

## Steps to reproduce
1. Shares → Shared with me, click "Hidden Shares" (URL gets `q_share-visibility=hidden`).
2. Press Back.

## Actual
URL is back without the param, but "Hidden Shares" stays selected and hidden shares are listed. Reload fixes it.

## Expected
"Shares" selected and visible shares listed.

## Cause (web v8.1.0)
`packages/web-pkg/src/components/Filters/ItemFilterInline.vue:62-71` reads the query only in `onMounted`; `SharedWithMe.vue:146,169-172` keeps the state in a local ref.

## Fix idea
Watch the route query in `ItemFilterInline` (same fix family as #3740).

## Environment
OpenCloud 8.1.0+85466427 (rolling), web 8.1.0, Chromium (Playwright), lynn, desktop. Found in the exploratory bug hunt of 2026-10-13 (opencloud-eu/qa, `exploratory/releases/hunt-2026-10-13`), finding H3-C2, reproduced 1/1+ (explorer).

Screenshots: `screens/H3-C2-H3-c2-hidden-filter-after-back.png` in the hunt folder.
